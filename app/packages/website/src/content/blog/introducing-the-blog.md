Infrawrench has grown a lot: dozens of provider plugins, a desktop app, a web app, a CLI, a mobile companion, an MCP server and a Terraform provider. Most of that has shipped quietly, with the [documentation](/docs) as the only record. This blog is where we'll talk about it out loud.

## What to expect

- **Product news.** New features, new providers, and what changed in each release.
- **Guides.** Walkthroughs of workflows that span more than one doc page. These live in their own [Guides](/blog#guides) section below the posts and stay out of the feed, so subscribing only brings you news.
- **Engineering write-ups.** The decisions behind how Infrawrench is built, and the occasional thing that went wrong along the way.

## Following along

Subscribe to the [RSS feed](/blog/rss.xml) in your reader of choice, or join the [community Slack](https://join.slack.com/t/infrawrenchllc/shared_invite/zt-3x6hs7pyf-riCnQeurmX_EDkv5Nan_Ig) to talk about anything you read here.

Like the rest of this site, every post is also available as markdown: append `.md` to its URL or send `Accept: text/markdown`.
